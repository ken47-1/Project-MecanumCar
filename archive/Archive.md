# Archive

Historical reference material. Nothing in here is part of the firmware build.

The original ZYC0044 kit firmware is not redistributed here. It is copyrighted by ZHIYI Technology and Ajay Huajian. Get a copy with the kit, or from the vendor's [software archive.](https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fo/ai2x0e1van34yfokd5ngr/h?rlkey=pgu0nurt40z5d0vs6wj1el8vi&e=2&dl=0)

What used to be here: the original single-file firmware and modified versions based on it. The modified versions were used during the early development of this project, before the firmware was completely rewritten with AI assistance and development continued on the new implementation. They were used to check motor direction codes, spin behavior, and other hardware details against the new implementation.

The hardware facts they contained (pin numbers, shift register bit patterns, direction codes) live as comments in the current source, where they belong.
